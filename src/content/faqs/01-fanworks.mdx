title: Can I make fanworks with your characters?
---

Yes, *please*. Fanfic, fanart, memes, meta, elaborate shipping charts...
literally anything (and we really do mean *anything*) your fannish heart
desires!

We're making these characters for fandom, and thus, **you** to play with.
Consider them your dolls, so go ahead and give your faves a happy ending, put
them through the wringer, and smash them together however you please.

We're *so* excited to see what you make. If you feel like sharing, tag us on
[Bluesky](https://bsky.app/profile/fujoweb.dev),
[tumblr](https://fujowebdev.tumblr.com/),
[Twitter](https://twitter.com/fujowebdev), or
[Mastodon](https://blorbo.social/@fujowebdev) so we can gush about it! Fics
can go in our very own
[AO3 tag](https://archiveofourown.org/tags/The%20Fujoshi%20Guide%20to%20Web%20Development%20-%20Essential%20Randomness/works),
too.
