title: What goes into designing the cast?
---

We bring the characters to life by combining programming concepts and our
favorite character tropes. We decide how characters fit together and develop
their looks, personalities and quirks to make them feel unique and memorable.

The research stage involves pulling bits from our own experiences and those
around us and, of course, going down plenty of rabbit holes. When we need a
perspective we don't have, we bring in outside consultants. For CSS, that meant
working with [Moon](/contributors/moon), our Mi'kmaq sensitivity reader, and a
deaf sensitivity reader who prefers to remain anonymous. You can see all our
collaborators on our [contributors page](/contributors).
