*All* the room. Our designs and character profiles are just a starting point,
not canon *per se*. They're not meant to limit your imagination, but to spark
your interest instead.

<figure class="gif">
  <img
    src={canonrules.src}
    width={canonrules.width}

    alt="GIF of Captain Barbossa from Pirates of the Caribbean saying 'The canon is more what you'd call guidelines than actual rules.'"
  />
</figure>

We welcome your takes on our characters' backgrounds, personalities, genders,
relationships; all of it. We can only hope these characters stick with you long
enough to inspire you that way.

For our part, we use he/him for the programming gijinka in official materials
as a nod to the genres that inspired FujoGuide. That's our convention, it
doesn't need to be yours!
